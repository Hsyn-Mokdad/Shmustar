About the town

The sun of the mountain

Chmustar (شمسطار) sits at 1,250 metres on the eastern slope of Mount Sannine, overlooking the Bekaa Valley between Baalbek and Zahlé.

It is a regional centre for dozens of surrounding villages, home to official institutions, schools, health centres and a busy market.
