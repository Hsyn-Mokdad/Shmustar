Nature & Tourism

Four real seasons, mountain air and views over the Bekaa Valley.

Why visit

A mountain town with four seasons

At 1,250 metres, Chmustar has cool summers, colourful autumns and snowy winters. Pines, cypresses and orchards surround the town.

  • Fresh mountain air and wide views
  • Snow in winter, golden fields in summer
  • Close to Baalbek's famous Roman temples (21 km)
Places to discover

What to see in Chmustar

Winter

Mount Sannine slopes

Snow-covered hills above the town, perfect for winter photos and walks.

All year

Bekaa Valley viewpoints

From the upper streets you can see the whole green plain of the Bekaa.

All year

The stone arch

The stone arch welcomes visitors and lights up at night during celebrations.

Spring

Ponds & springs

Small ponds fill with water in spring and reflect the trees around them.

Spring – Autumn

Caves & ancient wells

Caves and Byzantine-era stone wells and cisterns tell the story of the old settlement.

Summer – Autumn

Sunset walks

Golden sunsets over the fields make late afternoon the best time for a walk.

Visitor tips

Plan your visit

  • In winter, check the weather and carry snow chains.
  • Bring a jacket: evenings are cool even in summer.
  • Respect private fields and orchards.
  • Keep nature clean — take your rubbish with you.
