The Municipality

Who we are, what we do and how we are organised.

Our role

Serving the town every day

Chmustar Municipality is the local authority of the town. An elected municipal council takes decisions about local services, public works, the environment and town planning.

Chmustar is also the seat of the local Union of Municipalities, which brings the surrounding villages together on shared projects.

  • Clean streets and regular waste collection
  • Safe roads, lighting and snow clearing in winter
  • Fast and clear paperwork for residents
  • Support for education, culture and agriculture
Departments

How the municipality is organised

Administration

Registers requests, issues certificates and receives residents.

Finance & Fees

Collects municipal fees and manages the municipal budget.

Engineering

Studies building permits and follows public projects.

Public Works & Cleaning

Waste collection, roads, snow clearing and public gardens.

Municipal Police

Keeps order, organises traffic and applies municipal decisions.

Social & Cultural Affairs

Works with schools, associations and families in need.

Official institutions in town

Public offices in Chmustar

Because Chmustar is a regional centre, residents of nearby villages also come here for:

  • The Civil Status (Personal Records) Department
  • The General Security office
  • Social affairs services
  • Public schools and health centres
