Services & Paperwork

What you need, where to go, and how long it takes.

Main services

Choose a service

Each card lists the documents to bring, so you can finish in one visit.

Residence certificate

إفادة سكن — proves that you live in Chmustar.

You need:

  • Copy of your ID card
  • Proof of address (rent contract or title deed)
  • Paid fiscal stamp

Building permit

For new buildings, additions or major repairs.

You need:

  • Copy of the title deed
  • Plans signed by a registered engineer
  • Land survey (cadastral) map

Municipal fees

Annual fees on homes and shops (rental value).

You need:

  • Your property or shop number
  • Last year's receipt, if available
  • Payment at the finance office

Waste & cleaning

Report missed collection or request bulky-waste pickup.

You need:

  • Your street and nearest landmark
  • Type of waste
  • Phone number to reach you

Street lights & roads

Report a broken light, a pothole or a blocked road.

You need:

  • Exact location
  • A photo, if possible
  • Your contact details

Complaints & suggestions

Tell us what we can do better.

You need:

  • Use the contact form
  • Or visit the municipality
  • We reply within 5 working days

Good to know: requirements can change. If you are not sure, call the municipality before your visit.

Step by step

How to apply

  1. Check the documents you need on this page.
  2. Visit the municipality during office hours.
  3. Submit your request at the administration desk.
  4. Pay any fees at the finance office.
  5. Receive your document or a follow-up number.
