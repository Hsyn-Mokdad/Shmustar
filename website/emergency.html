Stay safe

Safety tips

In a snowstorm

Stay home if possible. If you must drive, use snow chains and tell someone your route.

In case of fire

Leave the building, close doors behind you and call 175. Never use the lift.

When you call

Stay calm. Say your name, the exact place and what happened. Do not hang up first.

Tip: print this page and keep it near your phone at home.
